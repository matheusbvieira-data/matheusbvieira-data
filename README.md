Data Analyst at Vivo (Telefônica Brasil), moving into Data Engineering. I work with telecom data at scale — billions of signaling records, 1TB+/year — and most of what I do comes down to the same thing: taking a process someone runs by hand every week and turning it into something that runs on its own, with logging, validation, and no one needing to remember to press a button.
